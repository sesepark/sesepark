# 박세환 · Sehwan Park

I'm an undergraduate at Seoul National University, majoring in Electrical and Computer Engineering. I entered the College of Liberal Studies in 2025.
I'm part of [SHAPE](https://github.com/snu-shape), a humanoid robotics team, where I work on robot teleoperation, demonstration data collection, and imitation learning.

## Interests

- Teleoperation for robotic arms and humanoids
- Demonstration data collection and imitation learning
- Control, data, and training tools for robotics experiments

## Robotics

### Team Projects

- **[MAICON 2026](https://github.com/sesepark/MAICON_2026_AirTiger) — 1st Overall · Minister of National Defense Award (최우수상)**<br>
  Our team, Air Tiger (에어타이거), won all six matches at the 2026 Military AI Competition finals. I developed LiDAR ICP-based localization, path planning, and obstacle avoidance, validated relocalization using recordings from the competition arena, and designed the ROS 2 node architecture and development interfaces.

- **[SHAPE-UP G1](https://github.com/sesepark/g1-project)**<br>
  Built the simulation environment, operator console, ROS 2 integration, and teleoperation on the physical robot using Unitree G1 and NVIDIA SONIC. Development notes and demos are available in the public repository.

- **[AI Worker Humanoid Challenge](https://github.com/sesepark/ai-worker-humanoid-challenge)**<br>
  Developed the teleoperation interface, camera integration, mobile base control panel, and leader-arm control for our team's ROBOTIS AI Worker project.

### Personal Projects

- **[SO-ARM101 Console](https://github.com/sesepark/soarm101-console)**<br>
  A console for controlling a robotic arm and recording demonstrations from a browser or phone. It also supports teleoperation by manipulating a 3D arm, without a physical leader arm.
- **[xArm7 Teleop](https://github.com/sesepark/xarm7-pi0-teleop)**<br>
  An experimental setup for teleoperating an xArm7 with a GELLO leader arm or phone, collecting demonstrations in LeRobot format, and using them for training and policy rollout with OpenPI pi0.
- **[sparkq](https://github.com/sesepark/sparkq)**<br>
  A queue for running GPU jobs sequentially on DGX Spark and Mac, with log, progress, and checkpoint management.

I review datasets and manage experiments in a personal Mac app I built.
My main tools are ROS 2, LeRobot, OpenPI, Python, and PyTorch.
